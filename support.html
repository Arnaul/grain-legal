照片与数据

拍的照片存在哪里？会上传到服务器吗？

照片保存在你自己的设备上，并写入系统「照片」App。Grain 没有任何图片上传功能，照片不会离开你的设备，我们无法查看、保存或分析你的照片。

删除 App 会删除照片吗？

不会。照片已经保存在系统相册里，删除 Grain 不影响这些照片。但 App 内的胶卷使用记录、偏好设置等本地数据会被一并清除。

在 App 内删除照片，系统相册里的那张还在吗？

不在。在 Grain 内删除照片时，会同时删除系统相册中对应的那一张，避免两张副本不一致。如果只想删除 App 内的记录，请勿使用删除功能。

导入的旧照片会不会覆盖原图？

不会。导入只读取照片用于渲染，成片会作为新照片保存，原图保持不变。

权限

为什么打不开相机？

请前往「设置 › Grain」，确认「相机」已开启。若之前选择过「不允许」，需要在这里手动打开。

为什么保存照片时提示无权限？

请在「设置 › Grain › 照片」中允许「添加照片」。若使用相册导入，还需要允许「读取」。

定位权限是必须的吗？

不是。定位只在你开启「位置信息」时使用，用于把拍摄地点写入照片的 EXIF。关闭后拍照、滤镜、相框等功能均可正常使用。我们不会记录或上传你的位置。

效果与成片

成片效果和实时预览不一致怎么办？

实时预览与最终成片使用同一套渲染管线。若差异明显，请把预览截图和成片一并发送给我们，便于定位问题。

带相框的照片边缘有白边或裁切异常？

请先确认已更新到最新版本。如果问题仍在，请告知具体的相框名称与设备型号。

导入的照片方向不对（上下颠倒或侧躺）？

请确认照片在系统相册中显示方向正常后再导入。若仍有问题，请把该照片发给我们用于测试。

订阅与退款

如何取消 Pro 订阅？

订阅由 Apple 管理，需在系统中取消：打开「设置 › Apple ID › 订阅」，找到 Grain 后选择「取消订阅」。取消后当前订阅周期结束前仍可使用 Pro 功能。

换手机后 Pro 权益没了怎么办？

用购买时同一个 Apple ID 登录，进入 Grain 的 Pro 页面，点击「恢复购买」即可恢复。

如何申请退款？

退款由 Apple 处理，我们无法代办。请访问 reportaproblem.apple.com，用购买时的 Apple ID 登录后提交申请。

订阅价格和周期是什么？

价格、周期与试用信息以 App 内 Pro 页面显示为准，该页面直接读取 App Store 的实时信息。

Photos & data

Where are my photos stored? Are they uploaded?

Your photos are saved on your own device and written to the system Photos app. Grain has no upload capability — your photos never leave your device, and we cannot view, store, or analyse them.

Does deleting the app delete my photos?

No. Photos live in your system library, so removing Grain leaves them untouched. Local app data such as film usage history and preferences will be cleared.

If I delete a photo in the app, is it removed from Photos too?

Yes. Deleting inside Grain also removes the matching photo from your library, so you never end up with inconsistent duplicates. If you only want to clear the in-app listing, do not use delete.

Will importing an old photo overwrite the original?

No. Importing only reads the photo for rendering. The result is saved as a new photo and the original stays untouched.

Permissions

The camera won't open. What should I do?

Go to Settings › Grain and make sure Camera is enabled. If you previously chose "Don't Allow", you'll need to turn it on manually here.

Saving fails with a permission error.

Go to Settings › Grain › Photos and allow "Add Photos". Importing from your library also requires read access.

Is location access required?

No. Location is only used when you enable the location option, to write the shooting spot into the photo's EXIF data. With it off, shooting, films and frames all work normally. We never record or upload your location.

Rendering

The final image doesn't match the live preview.

Preview and export share the same rendering pipeline. If you see a clear difference, send us both the preview screenshot and the saved image so we can investigate.

Framed photos have a white edge or look cropped.

Please make sure you're on the latest version. If it persists, tell us the frame name and your device model.

Imported photos come in upside down or sideways.

Check that the photo displays correctly in the Photos app before importing. If the issue remains, send us the photo for testing.

Subscriptions & refunds

How do I cancel Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions, select Grain and choose Cancel Subscription. Pro features remain available until the current period ends.

I switched phones and lost Pro.

Sign in with the same Apple ID you purchased with, open the Pro page in Grain and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple and we cannot issue them. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID and submit a request.

What are the price and billing period?

The Pro page inside the app always shows current pricing, period and trial details, read live from the App Store.
